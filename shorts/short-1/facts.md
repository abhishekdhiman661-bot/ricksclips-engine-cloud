Story type: real

Richard Lee McNair — career criminal serving two life sentences for a 1987 murder and robbery in North Dakota.
Source: Wikipedia (Richard Lee McNair), Grunge

First escape (1987): Used lip balm to grease his hands and slip out of handcuffs at a police station, then jumped from a third-story window.
Source: Grunge, Wikipedia

Second escape (1992): Climbed through a ventilation duct at the North Dakota State Penitentiary with two other inmates. Recaptured months later.
Source: Grunge, Wikipedia

Third escape (2006): From USP Pollock, Louisiana, a medium-to-high-security federal penitentiary. Assigned to repair torn mailbags in the prison's industrial shop.
Source: thetedkarchive.com, Wikipedia

Built a hidden compartment (escape pod) inside a pallet of mailbags. Included a breathing tube running to the outside. Pallet was shrink-wrapped by prison staff and loaded onto a forklift.
Source: thetedkarchive.com

Pallet shipped out of the prison gates to an off-site warehouse. During a lunch break, McNair cut his way out and walked away.
Source: thetedkarchive.com, Wikipedia

Shortly after the escape, a police officer stopped McNair jogging along a railroad track. McNair, wearing running gear, introduced himself as "Jimmy" and talked his way out of it. The encounter was captured on dashcam and became famous.
Source: YouTube dashcam footage (widely documented), Grunge, Wikipedia

McNair stayed on the run for over a year. Crossed into Canada. Recaptured by Canadian Mounties in New Brunswick in October 2007.
Source: Wikipedia

Currently held in federal supermax facilities under extreme lockdown.
Source: Wikipedia
